# Evidence And Confidence

## Canonical Claim States
- `OBSERVED`: direct source, test, runtime, command, configuration, or reviewed-document evidence.
- `INFERRED`: reasoned conclusion with stated limits.
- `PROPOSED`: candidate future direction.
- `DECIDED`: explicit project decision; implementation remains separately verified.
- `UNKNOWN`: evidence or decision is absent.

## Evidence Minimum
For durable claims preserve enough context to answer:
- what source supports this
- when and how it was observed or reviewed
- who/what produced the record where relevant
- validation state and confidence
- included, excluded, and intentionally untouched boundaries
- remaining contradiction or uncertainty

Confidence for inferred claims is `high`, `medium`, `low`, or `unknown`.

Generated reports and chat summaries are evidence, not authority. Promote findings only by reconciling them into the appropriate owner document or register. Missing evidence is not evidence of absence.

## Physical Limits
Do not infer spatial accuracy from sampling frequency. Dense temporal capture does not prove pavement-side, lane-level, or centimeter-level geometry. Record GNSS accuracy/vertical-accuracy evidence and uncertainty when trajectory quality matters.
